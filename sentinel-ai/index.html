FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does Sentinel AI detect hallucinations?

We combine token-probability analysis, self-consistency checks and retrieval cross-referencing to score every response in real time. Anything above your risk threshold is flagged, paused or routed to a human — your choice.

Will monitoring slow my agents down?

No. Sentinel runs asynchronously alongside your agents. Inline guardrails (like PII redaction) add under 50 ms; pure observability adds zero latency to your responses.

Which models and frameworks are supported?

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini and any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, plus LangChain, LlamaIndex and raw SDK instrumentation via our REST API and webhooks.

Is my data safe with Sentinel?

Yes. We use zero-knowledge architecture: payloads are encrypted at rest and in transit, PII is redacted before storage, and audit logs are immutable. We're SOC2 Type II certified.

Yes — you can adjust your plan at any point in the billing cycle. Upgrades are prorated instantly, while downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period.

We do. Registered non-profits and academic research groups receive 40% off any tier. Reach out via the contact page with your registration details.

Nothing breaks. New agents queue in a pending state and we notify you — you can archive an old agent or upgrade in one click.
