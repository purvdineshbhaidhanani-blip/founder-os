# Loop 5 — Home Maintenance Contractor AI

**Mission:** Build the world's smartest AI Home Maintenance Operating System. Not another contractor directory — an AI Home Maintenance Decision Engine.

## Phase 1 — Deep Understanding

**Problem:** Homeowners constantly ask: What maintenance should I do this month? Which contractor should I hire? Am I being overcharged? What repairs are becoming urgent? How much will this repair cost? Which appliances need servicing? Current solutions are fragmented (calendars, WhatsApp, Excel, paper notes, Google searches) — nobody gives a complete system.

**Customer**
- Primary: Homeowners, Apartment Owners, Villa Owners, Property Investors
- Secondary: Contractors, Electricians, Plumbers, HVAC Companies, Cleaning Services, Property Managers

**Pain score:** Missed Maintenance ★★★★★ · Finding Contractors ★★★★ · Repair Cost Confusion ★★★★★ · Emergency Repairs ★★★★★ · Warranty Tracking ★★★★

**Why they pay:** Prevent expensive repairs, know fair pricing, trusted contractors, emergency assistance, peace of mind.

## Phase 2 — Market Validation

**Existing solutions:** Thumbtack, Angi, TaskRabbit, HomeAdvisor, Google Sheets, manual notes.
**Common problems:** only contractor marketplace, no preventive maintenance, no AI recommendations, no repair forecasting, poor maintenance history.
**Market opportunity:** Become "The AI Home Maintenance Copilot."

## Phase 3 — Product Positioning

**Category:** AI Home Maintenance Operating System. NOT a contractor marketplace, reminder app, or task list.
**Core promise:** "We tell homeowners exactly what maintenance to perform, when to perform it, who to hire, and how much it should cost."

## Phase 4 — Core Value Engine

Property Profile → Asset Inventory → Maintenance Schedule → AI Risk Analysis → Repair Prediction → Contractor Recommendation → Daily Maintenance Guidance

## Phase 5 — Core Features

- **Property:** Rooms, Appliances, AC, Water Heater, Electrical, Plumbing, Roof, Garden

- **Repairs:** Repair History, Estimated Cost, Before/After Photos, Invoices, Contractors
- **AI:** Maintenance Coach, Repair Predictor, Cost Estimator, Contractor Assistant, Emergency Advisor

## Phase 6 — AI Features (daily)

## Phase 7 — Differentiation
